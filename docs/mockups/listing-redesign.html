Listing redesign — mockup, 21 Sept 2026. Real data from the screenshot. What changed, in order of what you will notice:
  1. A row is a meeting. One glyph (the source), the title, series, labels. No dots, no camera, no calendar tick, no film chip, no filename anywhere.
  2. The recording strip under the title says what the recording is and offers the one action that matters — “Ask Kawen to upload”, “Upload”, a live progress bar. It is silent on a plain transcribed meeting.
  3. One control per calendar row: “Add recording ▾” (menu shown open on the first row). Check / Upload / Recorder live inside it; Hide lives in the ⋯.
  4. Recordings tab (second card): recordings with no meeting yet. Uploads whose only name was a filename live here until linked or named — the archive never shows “…part1.mp4”.
  5. People are people. Initials + first name; “You”. Hover a row for ⋯ (Link to a calendar event, Temporary, Trash) and ›.
